🏛️ CityAssist Civic Mobile Platform

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to CityAssist ("we", "our", or "the Platform"). CityAssist is an intelligent civic service and public grievance management mobile application built to connect municipal residents, civic officers, and fleet logistics in real-time.

We are dedicated to safeguarding citizen privacy and handling personal data with absolute transparency, integrity, and strict adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

CityAssist collects only the minimum essential information required to deliver municipal grievance resolution and civic assistance:

Data Category What is Collected Purpose & Justification
📍 Geolocation Precise GPS coordinates (Foreground only) Critical Pinpoints the exact geographical location of civic complaints (potholes, garbage, broken streetlights) for municipal response teams. No background location is collected when the app is closed.
📸 Camera & Images Photos captured within the app Critical Provides visual verification and evidence of civic grievances. Photos are stored securely in Cloud Storage.
👤 User Profile Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address Account Authenticates citizen identity, provides official status updates via SMS/Notification, and verifies municipal staff authorization (Civic Officers and Drivers).
🔔 Notifications Device Push Notification Token Service Sends live progress updates when a complaint is acknowledged, in-progress, or resolved by the civic ward officer.
📱 Device & Network Network connectivity status, device OS version Technical Powers our offline-first SQLite synchronization, ensuring complaints submitted without internet connectivity automatically sync once back online.

2. How Your Information Is Used

  • Civic Triage: To route citizen complaints to the designated ward council officer and municipal department.
  • Evidence-Based Verification: To allow AI models and municipal inspectors to verify issue authenticity and avoid fraudulent reports.
  • Fleet Management: To assist municipal waste compactor drivers in navigating scheduled waste collection routes.
  • Emergency Broadcasts: To broadcast vital city advisories, municipal alerts, and citizen SOS requests.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We NEVER sell, monetize, rent, or trade citizen personal data to any advertisers or third-party marketing brokers.

Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Authorized Municipal Authorities: Ward officers and designated municipal maintenance crews responsible for resolving the reported civic issues.
  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase: Our secure infrastructure provider hosting database (Firestore), user authentication, and encrypted cloud storage.

4. Data Storage & Security Standards

All data transmitted between the CityAssist mobile application and our servers is strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Stored databases and media assets are secured using industry-standard AES-256 bit encryption at rest.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

Citizens have absolute control over their data under applicable data privacy laws:

  • Access & Correction: You may view and edit your profile details at any time directly in the app.
  • Complete Data Deletion: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account, mobile number, and complaint history. To request deletion, navigate to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account in the app or email us at siddhantramteke06@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Children’s Privacy

CityAssist is a general-audience civic service platform intended for residents and civic officials. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect enhancements in civic features or legal updates. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated effective date.

8. Non-Government Entity Disclaimer

Disclaimer: CityAssist is an independent civic technology platform designed to facilitate citizen grievance reporting, local service directories, and civic fleet monitoring. CityAssist is not an official government entity, does not represent any municipal corporation, state or central government department, and does not claim official governmental authority.
